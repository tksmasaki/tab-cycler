Tab Cycler

MRU (most recently used) tab cycling for Chrome.

Settings

How long to wait after the last shortcut press before switching to the highlighted tab.

Shortcuts

Configure keyboard shortcuts at chrome://extensions/shortcuts.

Defaults: Ctrl+Q (macOS: Control+Q) to cycle to the next recently used tab, Ctrl+Shift+Q for the previous. Esc cancels. Note that Ctrl+Tab is reserved by Chrome and cannot be reassigned.

Maintenance

Clears the recorded tab usage history and rebuilds it from currently open tabs.
